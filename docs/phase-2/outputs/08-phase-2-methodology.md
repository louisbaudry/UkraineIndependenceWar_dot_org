# Phase II Output 8 — Phase II Methodology

**Class:** METH (per DR-0046) | **Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** candidate —
effective upon founder approval of the Phase II consolidation
**Scope:** the research method by which Phase II — Theoretical Synthesis &
Standards Mapping — was performed, recorded per record §97 (methodology as
versioned research infrastructure) and the Phase II mandate's output 8.

## 1. Method

1. **Concepts before tables.** No workstream asked what database tables to
   create. Each asked which mature disciplines already possess the required
   concepts and which standards can faithfully express the Phase I
   requirements (the Phase II constraint, observed throughout).
2. **Fixed workstream sequence** (set in WP 0.1): preservation/provenance →
   historical/event knowledge → document identity/textual evidence →
   epistemology/evidence → argumentation → sanctions/export controls →
   governance/versioning. Later workstreams inherited earlier decisions as
   constraints, recorded in each paper's header.
3. **Per-workstream cycle:** discipline survey → concept-by-requirement
   mapping against the Phase I record → composition proposal → candidate
   conflict-register entries → candidate Decision Records → open questions →
   founder review → enactment of approved DRs → next workstream.
4. **Human decision on every DR.** All 52 candidate DRs were individually
   presented (interactive one-by-one review for WS1) or presented as
   recommended sets and explicitly approved by the founder (WS2–7), per
   record §78–79. No DR was enacted without founder approval; the approval
   wording is recorded in the DR register.
5. **Provenance discipline.** Every working paper and consolidation output
   carries an AI-provenance block (§80), SHA-256 fixity at deposit, and a
   provenance-register entry distinguishing uploads from AI-drafted
   documents. Superseded content is never silently rewritten (§77).

## 2. Sources and verification

- Primary requirement source: the Phase I Requirements Discovery Record
  (immutable deposit, `docs/discovery/`).
- Standards knowledge: the AI drafter's trained knowledge of the cited
  standards, with **targeted web verification of current versions** where a
  version claim mattered (OAIS/ISO 14721:2025; PREMIS 3.0; CIDOC CRM 7.1.3 /
  ISO 21127:2023; LRMoo 1.0; TEI 4.x line). Verification sources are cited in
  the relevant papers.
- Legal-structure descriptions (WS6) reflect the drafter's knowledge of
  UN/EU/US/UK frameworks as of early 2026; **primary-source legal texts were
  not systematically retrieved** in Phase II (collectors are a Phase III
  concern, Q-25).

## 3. Known limitations

1. **Single AI drafter, single reviewer.** All papers were drafted by one AI
   assistant and reviewed by one human (the founder). No external domain
   expert has yet reviewed the mappings; DR-0016-style independent
   reassessment (§83) has not been exercised. Mitigation: everything is a
   conceptual commitment, revisable by supersession before Phase III freezes
   anything physical.
2. **Approval depth varied.** WS1 DRs were reviewed item-by-item; WS2–7 DR
   sets were approved on recommendation. The register records which.
3. **Literature access.** Standards were mapped from training knowledge plus
   targeted verification, not from systematic literature retrieval of the
   underlying specifications. Claims about fine-grained standard internals
   should be re-verified against primary texts when Phase III consumes them.
4. **Speed over exhaustiveness in Phase II research breadth** — the record's
   §103 research list was covered at concept-map depth, not monograph depth;
   open questions (output 7) carry what remains.

## English

**Subject:** Question about preserving DeepStateMap's front-line data

Dear DeepState team,

Thank you for DeepStateMap. I run an independent, non-commercial historical
archive about Ukraine's war of independence, whose purpose is long-term
preservation of source material for later research. The project's working
files are public at
https://github.com/louisbaudry/UkraineIndependenceWar_dot_org.

I would like to preserve dated snapshots of the front-line map, using the
public data feed that the map itself loads (`deepstatemap.live/api/history/last`),
as a private archival copy. I could not find any terms of use or licence on
your site, so I am asking rather than assuming.

What the archive would do:
- Retrieve that one public feed by hand, occasionally (for now not more than
  once a day), and keep each snapshot unchanged, with a record of when and
  from where it was retrieved and a checksum.
- Keep it as a preservation copy. **It would not be republished, redistributed
  or modified, and nothing would be published without asking you first.**
- Credit DeepState wherever the data is cited.

Could you please tell me:
1. Whether you are happy for the archive to keep such a copy;
2. Whether you have preferred terms: attribution wording, a limit on how often
   to retrieve, or a preferred way of getting the data;
3. Whether any wider use (for example showing the map in the archive's public
   pages, with credit) would need a separate agreement, and whether you would
   be open to discussing it.

I am glad to answer any questions. Thank you for your work.

Kind regards,
Louis Baudry
Founder, Ukraine Independence War archive

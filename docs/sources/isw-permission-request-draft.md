**Subject:** Request for permission to archive the Russian Offensive Campaign Assessment

Dear ISW team,

I run an independent, non-commercial historical archive about Ukraine's war of
independence. Its purpose is long-term preservation of primary and secondary
material for later research. The project's working files are public at
https://github.com/louisbaudry/UkraineIndependenceWar_dot_org.

I would like to ask for ISW's written permission to preserve ISW's daily
*Russian Offensive Campaign Assessment* reports (the text and embedded maps
and graphics on each dated report page) as a private archival copy. I have read
your Fair Use and Attribution Policy (revised 8 January 2026) and I understand
that it requires written permission for incorporating ISW materials into other
datasets or systems. I would like to do nothing that the policy prohibits
without your agreement.

What the archive would do:
- Retrieve one dated report page per day, from the public ISW site, at a
  polite rate (one request per report), and keep the retrieved page unchanged,
  with a record of when and from where it was retrieved and a checksum.
- Keep it as a preservation copy only. **No redistribution, no republication,
  no modification, no bulk or API access for third parties, and no commercial
  or transactional use.** Nothing from it would be published without separate
  permission from you.
- Attribute ISW wherever the material is cited internally ("Source: Institute
  for the Study of War").

Could you please tell me:
1. Whether ISW permits this kind of private preservation copy;
2. Whether there are conditions (a rate limit, a delay after publication,
   attribution wording, or a preferred way of retrieving the reports);
3. Whether any broader use, such as quoting short excerpts with attribution in
   the archive's public pages, would need a separate request.

I am glad to answer any questions. Thank you for ISW's work.

Kind regards,
Louis Baudry
Founder, Ukraine Independence War archive

### EDIT-001 — The founder is final editorial authority; review becomes risk-tiered as the team grows
**Sources:** §78 · **Satisfied by:** DR-0046, DR-0063
**Verification:** *Inspection* — the DR register records founder approval on
every enacted DR; review tiers are documented and applied (SPEC-0002 §4).

### EDIT-002 — Substantive corrections leave a visible trace; nothing is silently overwritten
**Sources:** §77 · **Satisfied by:** DR-0055, DR-0064, DR-0077
**Verification:** *Test* — corrections create superseding records; no in-place
edit path exists outside governed redaction. *Audit* — sampled corrections
carry rationale, supporting evidence, editor, and effect on published
outputs.

### EDIT-003 — Consequential published conclusions are drafted against the Toulmin scaffold with calibrated qualifiers
**Sources:** §78 · **Satisfied by:** DR-0037, DR-0026, DR-0065
**Verification:** *Audit* — sampled consequential conclusions record claim,
grounds, warrant, qualifier (a registry likelihood band), and anticipated
rebuttals.

### EDIT-004 — Publication wording distinguishes designated from rule-derived, finding from assessment, quotation from paraphrase
**Sources:** §33, §62–63, §73 · **Satisfied by:** DR-0019, DR-0024, DR-0041
**Verification:** *Audit* — sampled published text preserves each distinction.
*Test* — published rendering derives its wording from typed data, so a
rule-derived applicability cannot render as a designation.

### EDIT-005 — Published representations of consequential conclusions are reproducible from their release baseline
**Sources:** §86; Principle 16 · **Satisfied by:** DR-0048
**Verification:** *Demonstration* — a published conclusion is reproduced from
its named release baseline, yielding the same text, evidence bundle, and
methodology and terminology versions.

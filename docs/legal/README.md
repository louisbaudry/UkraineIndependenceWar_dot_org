# docs/legal/

Documents addressed to, or arising from, **external legal advisers** — as
distinct from the project's own legal *layer* (the modelling of designations,
findings and restrictions), which lives in
[`REQ-LEGAL`](../requirements/REQ-LEGAL-legal-layer.md) and its decision
records.

Nothing in this directory is legal advice, and nothing in it is a controlled
document under [DR-0046](../decision-records/DR-0046-unified-document-control.md).
A document here proposes no policy and enacts nothing. When external advice
changes what the project does, the change is made in a Decision Record and in
the policy the advice bears on — never by editing a document in this
directory to say something new.

## Why the brief exists

POL-0001 is written by a non-lawyer and says so. Its §10 makes external legal
review a condition of the policy operating at scale, and suspends the §9
releases of the DR-0071 interim constraints until that review is recorded.
The review is therefore the single gate in front of the whole acquisition
plan — and an expensive engagement asked the wrong questions would leave the
gate shut anyway.

The brief's job is to make the questions specific enough to be answered once
and recorded, and to hand counsel the facts rather than the repository.

## Handling

This directory is public. Engagement particulars — the founder's identity and
address, counsel's name, fees, and anything the project holds above `public`
access tier — belong in the covering letter and the engagement terms, not in
a file here.

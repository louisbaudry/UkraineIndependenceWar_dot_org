# DR-0026 — Two-dimensional uncertainty model

**Category:** epistemology / methodology | **Status:** Approved | **Decided:** 2026-08-11 by founder/principal editor
**Origin:** CDR-W4-3, WP 0.5 §7/§10 | **Supersedes:** — | **Superseded by:** —

## Context

Record §42: probability and confidence are conceptually distinct; resist false
precision; research established approaches. WP 0.5 found intelligence practice
(ICD 203, PHIA) and IPCC practice independently converged on the same
two-dimensional structure.

## Alternatives considered

1. Calibrated verbal likelihood bands + separate analytic confidence (chosen).
2. Single numeric confidence score (rejected: WP 0.1 no-go; collapses §42's
   distinction).
3. Free-text uncertainty prose only (rejected: not comparable or queryable).

## Decision

Uncertainty is expressed in two dimensions, never collapsed:

- **Likelihood** of a proposition: one ordered scale of verbal probability
  expressions, each bound to an explicit numeric range. The exact scale
  (ICD 203 / PHIA / hybrid) is fixed at vocabulary enactment by a further DR;
  until then no probability wording is canonical.
- **Analytic confidence** in the judgment: low / moderate / high, derived from
  stated evidence quality, corroboration weighted by independence (DR-0028),
  and reasoning strength.

Prohibited: bare numeric confidence scores; probability values detached from
their band; averaging contradictory assessments (§40). Measurement uncertainty,
source disagreement, and analytical uncertainty remain typed separately.

## Consequences

- Estimative language becomes consistent, auditable, and translatable (§60).
- False precision is structurally blocked, not just discouraged.
- The band-definition DR is a standing Phase II obligation (WP 0.5 §11 Q1).

## Context

DR-0100 (2026-09-15) named the controller, for data-protection purposes, as
the founder/principal editor as a natural person, and left open — carried
from DR-0099 — "whether entity formation should itself be a preliminary
question for the review." The A6 legal-review brief's Q6 puts the same
question to counsel directly, and DR-0101 fixed what "recorded" means for
the POL-0001 §10 review: one specific act, not the receipt of advice. As of
this record, Part A of the brief has been sent to French data-protection
counsel and no response has been received (README.md, A6 status).

The founder was asked, one question at a time, whether to (a) wait for
counsel's answer to Q6 before deciding, (b) start entity formation now in
parallel with the pending response, (c) explicitly defer the question as a
standing ruling, or (d) skip it for now — and separately, if (b), which
legal form. The founder chose to start now, in parallel, as an **association
loi 1901**.

## Alternatives considered

**On timing**, three real options plus a decline:
1. **Wait for counsel's Q6 answer before deciding anything** — avoids the
   risk of forming an entity in a shape or jurisdiction counsel would not
   have recommended, at the cost of losing however many weeks counsel's
   response takes.
2. **Start entity formation now, in parallel with the pending response**
   (**chosen**) — accepts that the entity may later need restructuring if
   counsel's advice points elsewhere, in exchange for not blocking on
   counsel's timeline for a step the founder judged should proceed
   regardless.
3. **Record a standing ruling to defer, closing the open question without
   acting** — was available but not chosen; the founder chose to act, not
   merely to stop re-raising the question.

**On form**, given (b) was chosen: association loi 1901 (recommended, for
continuity with the France jurisdiction already named in DR-0099/DR-0100,
low cost, and fit for a documentation/archive project with no current
revenue or paid-staff plan); another French entity form (SAS, fondation);
a non-French entity (would reopen DR-0099/DR-0100's jurisdiction question);
or leaving the form undecided and recording only the intent. The founder
chose the association loi 1901 directly, so the "record intent only" branch
was not needed.

## Decision

1. **The project proceeds to form an association loi 1901**, the standard
   French non-profit association form, now — in parallel with, not
   conditional on, French data-protection counsel's pending response to
   Part A of the legal-review brief (including its Q6 on entity formation).

2. **This is a founder decision to proceed, not a completed formation.**
   What remains outside any session's reach and is not resolved by this
   record: choosing the association's name and object (*objet social*),
   drafting its statutes, filing the *déclaration* with the relevant
   préfecture (or online via `service-public.fr`), publication in the
   *Journal Officiel*, obtaining a SIRET from INSEE if the association will
   hold a bank account or receive funds, and holding the association's
   first general assembly (*assemblée générale constitutive*) to adopt the
   statutes and elect its first officers (*bureau*). These are the
   founder's or their counsel's acts, not a repository change.

3. **DR-0099/DR-0100's interim France jurisdiction is not reopened.** An
   association loi 1901 is a French entity; this record is consistent with,
   not a supersession of, the establishment jurisdiction already named.

4. **The controller identity named in DR-0100 Decision 2 (the founder as a
   natural person) stays accurate until the association is actually formed
   and takes over as controller.** That handover is itself the material
   change DR-0100 Decision 4 already contemplates under POL-0001 §11, and
   is recorded separately, when it happens, by a record that supersedes
   DR-0100's Decision 2 (and, in the same act, the parts of POL-0001 §10
   that restate it) — not by this record and not silently.

## Consequences

1. **Risk accepted, deliberately.** If counsel's eventual answer to Q6
   recommends a different form, jurisdiction, or timing, the association
   may need dissolving, restructuring, or leaving as a dormant shell while
   a different vehicle is formed. The founder chose to proceed regardless,
   the same trade DR-0100 made in choosing not to wait for counsel before
   naming a controller.

2. **This record does not by itself change the controller, the archive's
   hosting arrangements, or anything in POL-0001.** Those changes happen,
   if and when they happen, through the mechanism DR-0100 Decision 4 already
   set up — a POL-0001 §11 material-change review, recorded separately, once
   the association legally exists and is ready to take on the controller
   role (which needs, at minimum, its statutes adopted and a SIRET, so any
   Art. 28 processor contract with IONOS can be re-executed in the
   association's name rather than the founder's).

3. **An open question for the founder to carry to whoever handles the
   filing:** whether the association's *objet social* should name the
   archive's specific subject matter (Ukraine's Second War of Independence)
   or stay general enough to cover future scope changes without a statutes
   amendment. Not resolved here — it is drafting language for the
   statutes, not a governance decision this record needs to make.

4. **Nothing about collection, registration, or publication changes.** No
   source becomes registrable, Track B does not start, and LEGAL-009 stays
   "partially satisfied" exactly as DR-0100 Consequence 9 already states —
   starting entity formation is not a recorded §10 review.

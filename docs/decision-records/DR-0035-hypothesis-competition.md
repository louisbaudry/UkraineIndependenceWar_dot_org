# DR-0035 — Hypothesis competition is first-class

**Category:** epistemology / methodology | **Status:** Approved | **Decided:** 2026-08-11 by founder/principal editor
**Origin:** CDR-W5-4, WP 0.6 §2.7/§6 | **Supersedes:** — | **Superseded by:** —

## Context

Record §35: important investigations preserve hypotheses, supporting and
contradicting evidence, alternative explanations, discriminating evidence, and
revisions — avoiding confirmation bias by design. Heuer's ACH is the mature
instrument.

## Alternatives considered

1. First-class hypothesis sets; ACH matrices as derived views (chosen).
2. ACH as separate tooling/data (rejected: duplicates evidence relations that
   layer 3 already holds; views stay consistent, copies drift).
3. Hypotheses as prose notes (rejected: not queryable; loses discriminating-
   evidence typing).

## Decision

**Hypothesis sets are first-class research objects**: a set of competing
hypotheses (§30 category) for a defined question, with typed evidence
relations — supports / contradicts / **discriminates** / neutral — between
evidence and each hypothesis. **ACH matrices are derived views** over these
relations, not separate data. Negative findings (§76) and absence states
(DR-0029) participate as evidence.

## Consequences

- "Which evidence discriminates between hypotheses" is a query, not a
  spreadsheet.
- Hypothesis sets double as the structured research-question inventory
  (§74–75).
- Analyst revisions of hypothesis status are versioned assessments (DR-0026,
  §63).

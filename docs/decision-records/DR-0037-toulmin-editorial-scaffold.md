# DR-0037 — Toulmin as editorial scaffold only

**Category:** editorial / methodology | **Status:** Approved | **Decided:** 2026-08-11 by founder/principal editor
**Origin:** CDR-W5-6, WP 0.6 §2.1/§6 | **Supersedes:** — | **Superseded by:** —

## Context

Toulmin's model (claim, grounds, warrant, backing, qualifier, rebuttal) is a
proven drafting and review discipline but lacks the compositional rigor of the
adopted representation (DR-0032/0033).

## Alternatives considered

1. Toulmin as drafting/review scaffold for consequential publications (chosen).
2. Toulmin as the storage model (rejected: informal; the AIF-patterned
   representation is stored).
3. No prescribed drafting scaffold (rejected: the scaffold cheaply enforces
   that warrants and qualifiers are stated, which reviews need).

## Decision

Consequential published conclusions are **drafted and reviewed against the
Toulmin slots**: claim, grounds, warrant (the inference rule relied on),
qualifier (drawn from DR-0026's likelihood/confidence vocabulary), and
anticipated rebuttals. Toulmin is a **presentation and editorial scaffold
only**; the stored representation remains DR-0032's structure, from which the
Toulmin rendering is derivable.

## Consequences

- Reviewers see the warrant and qualifier explicitly — the two elements most
  often left implicit.
- The risk-tiered review model (§78) gains a concrete checklist shape for its
  higher tiers.

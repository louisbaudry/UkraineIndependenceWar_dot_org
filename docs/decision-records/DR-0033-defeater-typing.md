# DR-0033 — Defeater typing: rebut, undercut, undermine

**Category:** epistemology | **Status:** Approved | **Decided:** 2026-08-11 by founder/principal editor
**Origin:** CDR-W5-2, WP 0.6 §2.4/§6 | **Supersedes:** — | **Superseded by:** —

## Context

Record §34 requires visible inference; §38 distinguishes authenticity of an
object from veracity of its content; §40 preserves unresolved contradiction.
Structured argumentation provides the mature three-way defeater typology.

## Alternatives considered

1. Rebut / undercut / undermine typing (chosen).
2. Untyped "disputes" relation (rejected: collapses distinctions the record
   requires — a forgery claim and counter-evidence are different attacks).

## Decision

Conflict relations in the argument layer are typed:

- **Rebutting** — attacks the conclusion itself (counter-evidence);
- **Undercutting** — attacks the inference link, leaving premises intact (e.g.,
  the document is authentic but does not show what is claimed);
- **Undermining** — attacks a premise (e.g., the document is forged; the
  witness was not present).

Authenticity challenges (§38) enter as undermining; interpretation challenges
as undercutting. An attacked argument with no resolution remains visibly
contested (§40) — "unresolved" is a legitimate end-state.

## Consequences

- Verification workflows (Berkeley Protocol practice, DR-0008) record *which
  kind* of doubt was raised and answered.
- Analytic aids can search for unanswered defeaters without adjudicating
  (DR-0036).

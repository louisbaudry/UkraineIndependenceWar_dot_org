# DR-0072 — Adoption of the Personal Data Policy

**Category:** legal / editorial | **Status:** Approved | **Decided:** 2026-08-16 by founder/principal editor
**Origin:** CDR-P3-19, POL-0001 | **Supersedes:** — (fulfils record §13, LEGAL-009, Q-35) | **Superseded by:** —

## Context

Record §13 required a formal personal-data policy before broad automated
collection; DR-0071 bound interim constraints until it existed. POL-0001 was
drafted and its three open rulings put to the founder individually.

## Alternatives considered

1. Adopt with the §8 rulings and suspended releases (chosen).
2. Hold the whole policy at draft pending legal review (rejected: leaves the
   project with no operative personal-data standard while pipeline work
   proceeds).
3. Adopt with immediate release of DR-0071's constraints (rejected: the
   policy is drafted by a non-lawyer; releasing collection limits before
   legal review would rest a real-world risk on an unreviewed document).

## Decision

**POL-0001 v1.0 is adopted, effective 2026-08-16**, comprising:

- the **three-decision model** — preserve / structure / publish are
  independent decisions, with structuring the decision §13 exists to govern;
- the **never-auto-structure list**: Art. 9 special categories, Art. 10
  offence data about individuals (except authoritative records typed per
  §62–63), contact details and identity numbers, precise home addresses and
  vehicle registrations of private individuals, precise locations of living
  private individuals, and facial-recognition-derived identifications;
- the **category treatments** of §5 across officials, sanctioned persons,
  investigative subjects, combatants, victims, witnesses, minors,
  incidental civilians, and the deceased;
- the **data-subject request procedure** of §6, routing erasure through
  governed redaction with tombstones (DR-0055) and recording the basis for
  every refusal or limitation;
- the **operational safeguards** of §7.

**Founder rulings**, taken individually:

1. **§8.1 — already-public standard for victim identification.** The project
   never becomes the first publisher of a victim's name.
2. **§8.2 — documented-act standard for individual soldiers.** Public
   identification only on specific reviewed evidence of a specific act,
   typed as allegation absent a legal finding; never on presence or unit
   membership; Geneva III Art. 13 always applies.
3. **§8.3 — archiving/research primary legal posture** (GDPR Art. 89 with
   Art. 9(2)(j)), Art. 85 expression provisions secondary.

**Operative limit:** POL-0001 §9's releases of the DR-0071 interim
constraints are **suspended until the §10 external legal review is
recorded**. DR-0071 therefore continues to bind collection scope.

## Consequences

- The project has an operative personal-data standard governing all
  structuring and publication decisions from today.
- Collection at scale remains gated — the gate is now a **specific, actionable
  step** (external legal review in the establishment jurisdiction) rather
  than a missing document.
- On the review being recorded, §9's releases take effect on their stated
  terms and this DR is amended by a successor recording the outcome.
